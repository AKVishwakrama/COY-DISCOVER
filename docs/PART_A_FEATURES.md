## 1. Anonymous nudge for crushes who have not joined

**What it is.** When you add a crush who is not on Coy yet, you can send them one anonymous message through the invite link: "Someone added you on Coy. Join to find out if it's mutual." It never says who you are. Limit: one nudge per crush, and a daily cap per user.

**Why it is needed.**
- The product only works when both people are on Coy. Today the invite link needs the user to message the crush directly, which defeats the "stay secret" promise and people hesitate.
- A nudge solves the biggest drop-off: the user adds a crush, nothing happens, and they leave.
- It brings new users in through curiosity, which costs nothing.
- Risk to handle: spam and harassment. That is why it needs a one-per-crush limit, a block option for the receiver, and no wording that hints at who sent it.

## 2. A time-limited icebreaker after a match

**What it is.** After a mutual match, both people get one private prompt (for example "Pick one: coffee, walk, or just chat") and a 48-hour window to respond. If nobody replies, the match quietly fades.

**Why it is needed.**
- A match with nothing after it is a dead end. The most nervous users (the ones Coy attracts) will not send the first message.
- A shared prompt removes the "who goes first" fear.
- The 48-hour window creates a reason to act now and gives a clear retention loop.
- It also keeps the privacy story clean: nothing is exposed until both people choose to engage.
- Risk to handle: pressure. Let users extend the window once, and make "let it fade" a normal, guilt-free outcome.
